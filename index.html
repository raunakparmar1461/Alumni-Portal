ABOUT US

Building connections
beyond the classroom.

DCE Connect is the alumni networking platform of Darbhanga College of Engineering, created to bring alumni, students and faculty together.

The platform helps alumni stay connected with the DCE community while giving students opportunities to discover alumni, learn from their experiences and build professional connections.
